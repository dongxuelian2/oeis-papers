A214972 proposed FORMULA:
The jump conjecture is true: a(n)=#{r>=1:s(r)<=n}, where
s(1)=1 and s(r+1)=floor((3*s(r)+3)/2) are the A152009 terms.
Indeed a(n)>=r+1 iff floor(2*(n-1)/3)>=s(r), equivalent to
n>=1+ceil(3*s(r)/2)=s(r+1).

A152009 proposed FORMULA:
The proposed recurrence s(1)=1, s(r+1)=floor((3*s(r)+3)/2) is true.
After removing the first survivor, deleting positions 1 mod3 maps
old index j to 1+ceil(3*j/2); successive recorded first terms iterate this map.

A152009 proposed COMMENTS:
The convergence conjecture holds deterministically for every nonempty deletion
set with infinite complement. After r>=2*k-1 transforms the first k values
are exactly the triangular numbers. If the deletion set starts with the first
k triangular numbers, one transform preserves them and makes the next term
at least the (k+1)-st triangular number; a second transform makes that term
exact. This specifies the natural domain where infinite iteration is defined.
