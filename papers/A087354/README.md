# A087354: all-index existence of square concatenations

**Authors:** Qichao Wang (Hebei University of Technology); 董道豫 / Daoyu Dong (University of Electronic Science and Technology of China)
**Date:** October 17, 2026  
**Status:** complete constructive existence proof submitted for review. No claim to have determined the unknown OEIS minima or established literature priority.

## Theorems

For **every n >= 1**, there exist **strictly increasing nonzero perfect squares**

\[
s_1<s_2<\dots<s_n
\]

whose decimal concatenation \(s_1\Vert\cdots\Vert s_n\) is itself a perfect square.

This proves that every index has a value in all three OEIS sequences:

- [A087354](https://oeis.org/A087354): smallest square obtainable from n nonzero square blocks (repetitions allowed).
- [A391022](https://oeis.org/A391022): smallest such square from n **distinct squares in ascending order**.
- [A391189](https://oeis.org/A391189): least bound on the square roots of the n ascending square blocks.

These are **existence results only**, not formulas for the minimum terms.

## Exact square-extension lemma

Let \(X\ge1\). Because the multiplicative group of rationals of the form \(q=2^u5^v\) with \(u,v\in\mathbb Z\) is dense in the positive reals, select \(q>1\) with

\[
\frac1{\sqrt{10}}<\frac X2(q-q^{-1})<1.
\]

For **any sufficiently large h**, define

\[
A=X10^h/q,\quad B=X10^h q,\quad
Y=(B-A)/2,\quad Z=(B+A)/2.
\]

Choosing \(h\ge\max(|u|,|v|)+1\) guarantees A and B are even positive integers, hence Y and Z are integers. We have

\[
Z^2=X^2 10^{2h}+Y^2,\qquad10^{2h-1}<Y^2<10^{2h}.
\]

Thus the concatenation of \(X^2\) and \(Y^2\) is **exactly** \(Z^2\) and the appended nonzero square has exactly \(2h\) decimal digits. Once q is chosen, h can be increased arbitrarily, making the new block arbitrarily large. Induction yields strictly increasing square blocks of any length.

The proof generalizes to all bases having at least two different prime factors, via the density of the multiplicative group generated by those primes. Prime-power bases are not covered by this proof.

## OEIS comment suggestions (not submitted)

**A087354:** All terms exist. In fact, for every n >= 1 there are strictly increasing distinct nonzero squares s_1 < ... < s_n whose decimal concatenation is itself a square. This follows from the exact square-extension identity X^2 * 10^(2h) + Y^2 = Z^2, with Y^2 of precisely 2h digits, using a dense subgroup of decimal-smooth rationals. Proof: Q. Wang (2026), [paper URL].

**A391022:** For every n >= 1, a(n) is well-defined. Repeatedly extend any square prefix by an arbitrarily large square block while retaining a perfect-square concatenation. This permits strictly increasing square blocks for arbitrary n. No formula for the minima is claimed. Proof: Q. Wang (2026), [paper URL].

**A391189:** All terms are defined by the same infinite extension construction for strictly increasing distinct nonzero-square blocks. Proof: Q. Wang (2026), [paper URL].
