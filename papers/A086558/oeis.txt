Proposed COMMENTS for A086558:

No term is zero. For n >= 17, write n=C*p^e with p the largest prime factor,
d=length(n), L=n*10^(n-d) and U=(n+1)*10^(n-d). Dusart's explicit prime
interval bound supplies a prime q in ((L/C)^(1/e),(U/C)^(1/e)), and q>p,
so C*q^e has the required prefix, length, and signature. The remaining
indices are covered by fifteen small prime certificates. See Wang and Dong
(2026) for the complete inequalities and finite cases.

Proposed FORMULA:

For n=p^e a prime power, a(n)=nextprime(ceilroot(n*10^(n-length(n)),e)-1)^e,
where ceilroot(z,e) is the least integer w with w^e>=z and nextprime(z)
is the least prime strictly greater than z. Also a(1)=1.

For general n, Theorem 2 gives an exact finite formula: enumerate orderings
e_1,...,e_t of the exponent multiset and finite increasing prime tuples
p_1<...<p_(t-1); set C=Product p_i^e_i and
q=nextprime(max(p_(t-1),ceilroot(ceil(L/C),e_t)-1)). Minimize C*q^e_t<U.
For t=1 use C=1,p_0=1. Explicit bounds for every tuple are in the paper.
