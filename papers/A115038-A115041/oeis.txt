Proposed COMMENTS for A115038, A115039 and A115041:

The stated existence conjecture follows from ((p-1)/2)^2+p=((p+1)/2)^2
for every odd prime p, and 5^2+2=3^3 for p=2. Thus all three sequences
are defined. In particular A115038(1)=5, and
A115038(n) <= (prime(n)-1)/2 for n >= 2.

Proposed FORMULA for A115038:

For odd prime p, put U=(p-1)/2 and B=(p+1)^2/4. The minimum x is the
minimum of {U} and the positive square roots of y^r-p which are integers,
where r runs over odd primes <=floor(log_2(B-1)) and
ceilroot(p+1,r) <= y <= floorroot(B-1,r). The unique square witness is U;
every smaller perfect-power witness must have an odd prime exponent.

The corresponding maximal exponent is the gcd of the prime exponents
in x^2+p; its primitive integer root gives A115041.
