Proposed COMMENTS for both A088862 and A090335:

The existence conjecture holds. More generally, in every integer base b >= 2,
every prescribed prime signature has infinitely many representatives whose
divisors greater than 1 form a prefix code. If the exponents sum to r >= 1,
one representative is less than b^(r^3+32*r). Choose the prime factors in
(b^(k-1/r), b^(k-1/(r+1))) with k >= r^2+32; the proof and the explicit
prime-interval estimate are given by Wang and Dong (2026).

Indexing correction for editorial review:
Both displayed lists and the synthesized b-files start with 1, corresponding
to zero prime factors. Their names, formulas, and worked examples use the
factor count as the index. Change OFFSET from 1,2 to 0,2 to align them.
The theorem proves existence for every factor count independently of this edit.
