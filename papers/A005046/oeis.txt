Proposed update for https://oeis.org/A005046 (not submitted).

The eventual-periodicity conjecture is true for every modulus m. Put
P_0=1 and P_{r+1}=(c*d/ds+s*d/dc+s)P_r. Then a(n)=P_{2n}(0,1).
Modulo m, the operator descends to the finite ring
(Z/mZ)[s,c]/(s^m,c^m-1), so its orbit is eventually periodic.
For m=2, P_6=P_0 in this quotient; for m=5, P_6=P_2.
Consequently the stated period 6 modulo 10 holds from n=1.

The Bessel formula is also true. Expanding exp(e^x/2)*exp(e^-x/2)
gives exp(cosh(x))=sum_{k in Z} I_|k|(1)*exp(k*x). Locally uniform
absolute convergence of every derivative justifies differentiation 2n
times at zero, giving the stated identity for n>0.

Proof source: main.tex and main.pdf in this directory. Original conjectures
remain credited to Peter Bala (2023) and Velin Yanev (2026).
