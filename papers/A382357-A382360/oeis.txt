Proposed COMMENTS for A382357:

The permutation conjecture holds. In fact, for every integer b>=2 and fixed
jump d>=1, every finite legal seed of distinct positive integers has a greedy
completion which is a permutation of its compatible valuation class
v_b(n) == r (mod d). The proof uses the last exits of the valuation levels.
For b=2,d=1, hypothetical escape would give positive integer crossing counts
satisfying 4*U_k <= U_(k-2)+U_(k-1)+1. Consecutive pairs of these counts
strictly contract, a contradiction. See Wang and Dong (2026), Theorem 1.

Remove the empirical qualifier from a(2*n+1)=a(2*n)+1, n>=0.
