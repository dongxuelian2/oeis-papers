The proposed exponential formula and its coefficient recurrence hold
at every order. Let G(x)=sum a(n)x^n. Logarithmic Stirling expansion
gives log G(x)=sum(j>=1,b(j)*x^j/j), where
2/(1+exp(4z))=sum(j>=0,b(j)*z^j/j!). Explicitly
b(j)=2*(1-2^(j+1))*4^j*Bernoulli(j+1)/(j+1) for j>=1.
The positive even b(j) vanish, so G(x)*G(-x)=1.

All3functional conjectures for H(x)=x*G(x) are true as formal
identities. Writing F(t)=t*(Gamma(t+1/2)/Gamma(t+1))^2 gives
F(t)*F(t-1/2)=(t-1/2)/t. With x=1/(8t), this proves
H(x)*H(x/(1-4x))=x^2. Oddness of log G proves
H(x)*H(-x)=-x^2; combining these gives
H(x)=-H(-x/(1+4x)). No convergence is required.
