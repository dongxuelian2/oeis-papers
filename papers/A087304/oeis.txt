Proposed COMMENTS for A087304:

No term is zero. In fact, for every n there are infinitely many eligible
multiples. Write n=2^a*5^b*m with gcd(m,10)=1, put s=max(a,b), and choose
1 <= t <= m such that R_t=(10^t-1)/9 is divisible by m. Then
(n*10^(j*t)+R_(j*t))*10^s is an eligible multiple for every j >= 1.
This extends the standard coprime repunit construction recorded in A340204.

The exact minimum is given by the finite digit recurrence in Wang and Dong
(2026), Theorem 2. If P is the product of the nonzero digits of n and d is its
digit length, the formula needs at most B=n*tau(P)*(d+2) digit steps. States
record remainder mod n, the unused divisor of P, and the mismatch state for
the forbidden regular language digits(n)0*. On an edge labeled a, update
D_(l+1)(v)=min(10*D_l(u)+a); minimize over accepting states and 1 <= l <= B.
