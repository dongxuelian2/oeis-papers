COMMENTS
For every n>=1, a(n)=n+1 if n+1 is prime at least 5, and a(n)=1 otherwise. This proves the official converse conjecture and the prime-indicator identity.

CORRECTION / SCOPE
The single-sum observation is false without qualifications: n=7 gives 8 dividing 2^7+7!, although 8 is composite.
