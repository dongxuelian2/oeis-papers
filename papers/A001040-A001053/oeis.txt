A001040 COMMENTS
Kurkov's November 19, 2019 rook-tour conjecture holds. Classify directed open tours by the odd number 2k+1 of moves between the two columns. The count for this class is 2*n!*(n-2*k-1)!*binomial(n-k-1,k)^2. Summing gives 2*n!*a(n). The finite coefficient sum agrees with Barry's formula already recorded in the entry; its defining recurrence follows termwise from (N-k)^2-N*(N-2*k)=k^2.

A001053 COMMENTS
Kurkov's November 19, 2019 rook-tour conjecture holds. Classify directed open tours by the even number 2j of moves between the two columns. The count for this class is 2*n!*(n-2*j)!*binomial(n-j,j)*binomial(n-j-1,j-1). Summing gives 2*n!*a(n). The finite coefficient sum agrees with Bala's formula already recorded in the entry; its defining recurrence follows termwise from (n-j)*(n-j-1)-(n-1)*(n-2*j)=j*(j-1).

Both counts treat reversal as a distinct tour. The proof applies to all n>=1 and handles n=1 explicitly. Related closed-tour results are credited to Max Alekseyev's 2024 MathOverflow answer; no new claim about those earlier results is made.
