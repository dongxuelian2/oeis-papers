Both conjectures are true. The right diagonal is the binomial
transform y_j=sum(i=0..j,binomial(j,i)*b_i) mod2 of the input
bits read least significant first. The Pascal transform is its
own inverse over F2: its square has entries binomial(j,i)*2^(j-i).

Output one therefore has unique inverse with only the leading
input bit one, proving a(n)=1 iff n is a power of2. For L>=2,
output 2^L-1 has invalid inverse of length1. Output 2^L-2 has
unique valid inverse n=2^(L-1)+1, giving the block maximum.
Thus strict record positions are1 and2^m+1 for m>=1, with values
1 and2^(m+1)-2.
