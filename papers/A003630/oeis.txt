A003630 update draft, 2026-10-27

The March 26, 2017 product conjecture is true, with factors taken
in increasing prime order. Let chi_4 and chi_-3 be the primitive
real characters modulo 4 and 3, respectively, and let
L_X(1,chi)=product(p<=X, (1-chi(p)/p)^(-1)). For every X>=3,

product(p<=X, p=5 or 7 mod12, ((p-1)/(p+1))^chi_4(p))
 = (9/8)*L_X(1,chi_-3)/L_X(1,chi_4).

For p>3 the local factors agree. The factors at p=2 and p=3 on
the L-product quotient are 2/3 and 4/3, giving the correction 9/8.
The prime-number theorem for arithmetic progressions gives ordinary
increasing-prime convergence of the nonprincipal L-products at 1.
Since L(1,chi_-3)=pi/(3*sqrt(3)) and L(1,chi_4)=pi/4,
the limit is sqrt(3)/2.

Full proof, including convergence justification, and exact finite
Euler-factor verification: papers/A003630/main.tex and verify.py.
The stable manuscript URL is to be pinned to the commit containing
the checked-in main.pdf before this draft is used for submission.
No OEIS submission has been made.
