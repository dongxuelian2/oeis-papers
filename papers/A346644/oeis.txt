Proposed COMMENTS for A346644:

Every term exists. Write n=2^a*m with m odd, set u=2^a, A=2^u-1, h=m*A,
and choose an odd prime p == -1 (mod n), which exists by Dirichlet's theorem.
Then K=2^(u-1)*p^(h-1) has sigma(K)/tau(K) = (1+p+...+p^(h-1))/n,
whose numerator is 1 modulo n. Its reduced denominator is n.

Proposed FORMULA:

a(1)=1. a(2^s)=2^(2^s-1) for s >= 0.
For odd prime q, let ell_i(q) be the i-th prime not congruent to 1 modulo q.
Then a(q^s)=min_{b_1>=...>=b_t>=1, sum(b_i)=s}
Product_{i=1..t} ell_i(q)^(q^b_i-1), for s >= 1.
In particular a(q)=2^(q-1) for every prime q.

Proposed EXAMPLE:
a(81)=57600, from the partition (2,1,1) of 4, giving 2^8*3^2*5^2.
The five partition candidates for exponent 4 are compared in the paper.
