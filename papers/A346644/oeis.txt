Proposed COMMENTS for A346644:

Every term exists, with an elementary witness: write n=Product q_i^a_i
(q_i increasing), let ell_i be the i-th prime, and set U_0=1,
b_i=a_i+v_(q_i)(sigma(U_(i-1))), U_i=U_(i-1)*ell_i^(q_i^b_i-1).
Then the reduced denominator of sigma(U_s)/tau(U_s) is n.

Every witness K has a divisor K' with the same denominator such that,
for each prime-power factor p^(m-1) of K', m has only prime factors
dividing n and gcd(m,1+p+...+p^(m-1))=1. Consequently every a(n) has
this normal form. Its odd part is always a square, and a(n) is a square
when n is odd. For 2^a || n, a>=1, v_2(v_2(a(n))+1)=a.

Proposed FORMULA:

a(1)=1. For n>1, let U be the elementary witness above and consider every
tuple of distinct primes 2<=p_1<...<p_t<=U, 1<=t<=floor(log_2(U)).
Start m_i=1. Process primes q|n in increasing order. At each stage set
G={i:p_i=q} union {i:p_i not congruent to 0 or 1 mod q, ord_q(p_i) does not divide m_i},
N={i:p_i not congruent to 0 or 1 mod q, ord_q(p_i) divides m_i},
B=v_q(n)+Sum_{i in N} v_q(p_i^ord_q(p_i)-1).
Branch over every allocation b_i>=0 (i in G) with Sum b_i=B, replacing
m_i by m_i*q^b_i on G. An empty G gives no branch. For q=2, simply
G={i:p_i=2}, N=empty, B=v_2(n). Then a(n) is the minimum of all final
products Product p_i^(m_i-1). Every such product has denominator n,
and the normal-form theorem proves the minimum is represented.
This is a finite arithmetic minimization formula, not an elementary
expression without minimization. Any verified witness may replace U.

a(2^s)=2^(2^s-1) for s>=0. For odd prime q, let ell_i(q) be the i-th
prime not congruent to 1 modulo q. Then
 a(q^s)=min_{b_1>=...>=b_t>=1, sum(b_i)=s} Product ell_i(q)^(q^b_i-1).
In particular a(q)=2^(q-1) for every prime q.

Proposed EXAMPLE:
a(30)=115200=2^9*3^2*5^2, with tau=90 and sigma=1023*13*31;
the gcd of sigma and tau is 3. Cross-factor cancellation is essential.
a(40)=6480000, a(42)=1843200, a(45)=3600, a(60)=4706440200.
a(81)=57600, from the partition (2,1,1) of 4, giving 2^8*3^2*5^2.
