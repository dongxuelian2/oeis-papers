The conjecture is true. Every twin-prime pair (p,p+2) other than
(3,5) is (6m-1,6m+1), hence its product is 36m^2-1=-1 mod9.
Decimal digit sum preserves residue modulo9, proving every
noninitial term is one less than a multiple of9. The exceptional
first pair gives digit sum6. No assumption of twin-prime infinitude
is needed. Proof and full5000-term official-data comparison are
in papers/A136050/main.tex and verify.py.
