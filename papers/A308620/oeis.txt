The conjectured GF is exact. The conjectured signature applies from
n=12, with a one-unit exception at n=11 (actual42, predicted41).

Proof: let D=(1-x)^2-x^5 and E=1-x^2-x^5. Summing the five
binomial components Q,R,Rbar,S,Sbar in Burger et al., Theorem3,
Eq4.2 gives respectively x^4/D, x*(1-x)/D, x*(1+x)/E,
x^7/((1-x)*D), x^7/((1-x)*E). Here the barred sums use
sum(j>=0,binomial(floor(j/2)+h,h)*x^j)=(1+x)/(1-x^2)^(h+1).
The full GF is x/(1-x)+Q+(R+Rbar+S+Sbar)/2, which simplifies
to the rational expression conjectured in the entry. Equivalently,
(1-2x+2x^3-x^4-2x^5+2x^6+x^10)*G(x)
=2x-2x^2-2x^3+3x^4-4x^6+x^7-x^9+x^11.
