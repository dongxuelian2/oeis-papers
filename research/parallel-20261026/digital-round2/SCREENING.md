# Additional digital screening, round two, 2026-10-26

These twelve distinct entries were inspected through their full
official pages on 2026-10-26, beyond the first-round eleven digital
entries. Search snippets omitted a completed proof in A061101, so
that candidate was explicitly rejected rather than counted.

| Official source | Exact statement / source status | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| [A136050](https://oeis.org/A136050) | Harvey P. Dale, 2016-12-02, conjectures every noninitial twin-prime-product digit sum is one less than a multiple of nine. | **Completed** whole congruence conjecture: twins greater than three are 6m±1, product 36m²−1, and digit sums preserve residue modulo nine. This is not a claim about infinitude of twins or occurrence of every value. |
